# Art direction and identity — THE RELAY BELOW
Status: approved direction only after owner sign-off; no final assets yet.

## What this must look like
Industrial British coastal realism with a strange mid-1990s analogue culture. The contrast is between bureaucratic precision and living instability: immaculately numbered terminals against corroded salt-fog surfaces, straight conductor lines against the Return's uneven ceramic resonators. Scares are built from silhouettes, obscured rooms and shapes that have a function.

Palette and purpose:
- Wet carbon #111915 — deep shadows, never flat black.
- Oxidised green #34463E — painted machine housings and information marks.
- Bone paper #DDD5C1 — reports and main text.
- Signal amber #D18A43 — operating indicators; sparingly used for interactability.
- Rust red #793A35 — emergency markings; not global horror overlay.
- Salt white #AAB9B5 — wet highlights, seawater exposure.

## Typography
Never use an off-the-shelf centred neon-glow logo. Create an ORIGINAL vector title lockup made of custom cut-metal stencil letters, with 'THE RELAY' compressed above the much narrower word 'BELOW' and deliberately missing small strokes on repeating E/L forms. The title should be authored as an image/decals with proof of original design; a supported Roblox text font will be used for surrounding text.
- Operational UI: Roblox-supported RobotoCondensed or TitilliumWeb, medium weights, with manual tracking/spacing where needed.
- Labels/logbook numerals: Roblox-supported RobotoMono.
- Handwritten archival annotations: carefully placed original scans/illustrations; not an entire screen of typewriter novelty font.
- Font families must be verified in Roblox Studio. Prioritise readability and caption fallback.
Roblox font reference: https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/enums/Font

## Screen layout
Desktop 16:9 reference. Main title occupies upper-left ~44%, behind a physically modeled relay board. Vertical section numbers at extreme left; menu items align with feed-slot marks. Right 1/3 reserved for moving industrial environment. A small print-head advances when choosing buttons. Minimal animation and high contrast. Use no huge rounded pill buttons, stock bright-blue gradients, neon outlines, random glitch fonts or repeated skull motifs.

Main menu states: title/Begin Shift, Crew, Records, Settings, Credits; mouse, keyboard, controller and touch; safe margins for mobile; toggle sound, caption size, flashes, reduce motion, microphone opt-in and mic sensitivity. Intro can be skipped. Interface transitions are timed to physical relays, but menus remain fast and accessible.

HUD shows task card only when needed, lower-left equipment metadata and contextual tap/hold icons. Diegetic status: oscilloscope, indicator lamps, physical logbook, room signs. Threat is communicated by nearby contacts sparking / relays clicking, with optional accessibility pulse indicator. Avoid permanently showing enemy distance or a big red 'MIC NOISE 100%' gauge.

## Environment model and textures
Architecturally plausible rooms and visible cables leading to systems the player uses. Avoid giant featureless corridors: use angled sightlines, partial glass, trussed overhead ducts, grates, identification plates and seawater damage. Blend trim-sheet metal, baked subtle rust and low-cost wet decals with carefully placed dirt. Props should have tiny plausible design details: numbered tag screws, rotary isolators, oil inspection marks, faded coastal charts, original typewritten logs. No off-the-shelf Roblox horror kit or copyright-unverified toolbox model as final asset.

## Creature visual rules
Distinct custom meshes, not spheres/blocks glued to an R15 avatar. Concept sheets: frontal silhouette, profile, surface detail and rig joint map. High fidelity in key silhouette; optimise peripheral detail. The Return does not need gratuitous blood. Animation uses exaggerated stillness, compressed folds, minute diaphragm movement and one decisive step instead of constant cartoon lunging.

## Cover art and icon
Illustrated by hand or assembled from original in-engine photography and authored graphic design (not generic AI-generated art). Icon: ceramic listening horn emerging from an almost black square, single copper wire forming an incomplete circle; title version uses the stencil lockup. Thumbnail: a human maintenance worker in the distance beneath monstrous out-of-focus cables, strong spatial composition. Add clear production proof, Roblox asset IDs and licenses to docs/ASSETS.md.

## Reference use
Use real industrial archive photography for materials, wall hardware and lens language only. Do not copy specific photographers' images into shipped assets without permission. Collect mood references separately from game assets.
