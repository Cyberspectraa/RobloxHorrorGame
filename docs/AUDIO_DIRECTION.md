# Original audio direction — THE RELAY BELOW
Status: design only; no licensed/finished master recordings in repository yet.

## Musical identity
Audio must feel infrastructural first and scored second. No generic copyright-free 'epic scary strings' or constant horror stingers. Establish one 3-note motif in relay clicks and very low bowed-metal tones. Sparse harmonic material in four situations:
1. Title screen — damp relay clicks, 46–52 Hz-like sub-bass impression kept mobile-compatible via harmonics, brief tape printhead, distant wind. Music almost absent.
2. Exploration — low held harmonics entering and receding around the actual machine loops; long pockets of silence.
3. Hunt — percussion primarily generated from the Return's physical rhythm and machinery, not fast bombastic orchestra; crescendos follow AI state rather than fixed timestamps.
4. Finale — motif made melodic for the first time, arrangement adapted per ending; result screen is quiet.

All tracks original or properly licensed for Roblox. Stems ideally: DRONE, METALLIC, PULSE and AIR; mixer crossfades with attack/release and prevents stacking multiple combat loops.

## Ambience by region
Gantry: wind around guide wires, tide thudding concrete, rain droplets in exposed housings.
Switchgear: oil-filled transformer hum, low 50 Hz-like buzz impression, occasional contactor clicks, switch lever contact noise.
Archive: soft tape capstan hiss, ventilation, worn rollers and muffled cross-room relay taps.
Galleries: the most controlled quiet; faint room tone, distant tonal resonance, rusted rotating dishes.
Intake: pressurised water, dripping into different materials, valves and metal rattles.
Chamber: mechanical air pulse, cable tension and sound sucked away before the crucial choice.

3D positional audio and reverb zones must make rooms recognizable. Occlusion approximated by room graph and door state, but no expensive per-frame raycasting to all sources.

## Threat audio grammar
The Return's three relay knocks are identifiable and non-deceptive: distance, material reflections and rhythmic variants reveal its state. It produces a short answering knock to a high-confidence noise event, but not on every report. Hearing is primary drama; accessibility indicator mirrors danger state with a subtle glyph/edge ripple, not a perfect enemy map.

Important effects list: crank click, metal grate steps, wet concrete steps, closing steel door, glass partition creak, isolator handle, spool insertion, oscilloscope hum, dish bearing friction, pressure valve, water surge, decoy rattle, threat capture, tape/equipment handling, switchboard printing. Each effect gets variations, layered physical sounds and consistent loudness.

## Mix and player settings
Independent music/ambience/effects/voice controls; default avoid distorted high peaks and tiring constant low frequency. Sidechain attenuate ambience during story lines and critical puzzle feedback. Captions identify direction and source (e.g. '[three relay knocks — behind the archive wall]') with adjustable size/background, no false precision. Visual puzzle equivalents for pitch and pulse; reduced jump-scare and camera shake toggles.

Deliver assets as original uncompressed masters and Roblox-compatible exports as appropriate. Record audio licensing, edit provenance, durations, loudness target and sound IDs in docs/ASSETS.md.
